Nova Control: project details +

What I built

Recipe versioning, dispatch, material tracking, production reports, and concrete-passport PDFs, with Delta and legacy Omron connections. A simulated controller supports testing without plant hardware.

Safety boundary

The desktop application sends recipe and order data to the PLC. The operator checks it and starts production from the plant touchscreen. The software records the results; it cannot start, stop, reset, or directly control the machinery.

Testing and status

Tests exercise real SQLite data, UI interactions, and simulated production. Boundary checks caught invalid-number defects; PDF checks caught a Cyrillic text bug. Version 2.0.4 is feature-complete, with real-hardware verification and Bulgarian-operator acceptance testing still pending.

Fake Discount Bulgaria: project details +

What I built

On-page price charts, explained verdicts, price targets, searchable history, and backup/restore, with Bulgarian and English interfaces.

How it works

Tracking begins when a user visits a supported product. Verdicts show the observations behind them and stay in a tracking state when history is insufficient. Local storage drives the result; the public build also uploads price observations to Supabase.

Testing focus

Saved retailer pages support repeatable checks of price extraction, dynamic navigation, and incorrect product matches. The history reflects recorded visits, rather than every product or price in the market.
